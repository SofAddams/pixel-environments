# Pixel Environments

Generate animated pixel-art scenes — rain, flames, snow, seasons, campfires — and put your own customizable pixel character in them.

**Try it:** https://sofaddams.github.io/pixel-environments/

- 10 scene presets plus full control over season, time of day, weather and effects
- A character creator: body, skin tone, hair, face, outfit, accessories, mobility aids
- 13 poses, including sitting by the campfire, playing guitar and sleeping
- Share links that save your scene and character, so anyone can open and remix them
- Export GIFs, sprite sheets, PNG frames, avatars and character sprite sheets

No build step and no dependencies: open `index.html` or serve the folder with any static server.
